### A bracket after a multisig threshold is the multisig's fault

`parse` named the key count for `wsh(multi(1)(,K))`, where Bitcoin Core
reads the threshold, expects a `,`, and refuses the bracket it finds
instead. `multi()`, `sortedmulti()`, `multi_a()` and `sortedmulti_a()` now
name the bracket after the threshold, leaving a threshold Core cannot read
as the fault named first, and a multisig with no key and no bracket as it
was (closes #116).
