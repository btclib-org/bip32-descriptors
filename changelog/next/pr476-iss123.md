### A Miniscript subexpression that is no Miniscript is refused

`Miniscript` checked the number of its `subs` and not their type, so a
subexpression that is not a `Miniscript` failed later with a builtin
`AttributeError` on `_static_ops`. Each element is now asserted a
`Miniscript` when the node is built, leaving a `BTClibTypeError` that names
the field and the type alone (closes #123).
